Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, which ones the Ayra Studio website uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

Last updated: see footer of this page

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store on your device. They let the site recognise your browser between requests or between visits. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags and SDKs, which serve a comparable purpose. In this policy we use the word "cookie" to mean any of these technologies.

2. The cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies. These cookies are required for the site to work. They keep your session alive, remember your form draft, or remember your cookie preferences. The site cannot function properly without them.

Analytics cookies (if enabled). These cookies help us understand how visitors use the site — which pages are read, where users drop off, and which links are clicked. The information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Advertising cookies. These cookies are set by Google AdSense and similar partners. They are used to deliver ads on this site, to limit how often you see a given ad, to measure ad performance, and — where you have not opted out — to make the ads you see more relevant to your interests. Advertising cookies may also be set when you visit other sites that participate in the same advertising networks.

3. Google AdSense and personalised advertising

AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of certain third‑party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info (for users in certain regions) or by using the controls in your browser or mobile device.

4. How long cookies last

Some cookies are "session" cookies and are deleted when you close your browser. Others are "persistent" cookies and stay on your device until they expire or you delete them. Most advertising cookies we work with last between 30 days and 24 months, depending on the partner and the specific cookie.

5. How to control cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Most browsers also let you block cookies from specific sites. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may mean parts of the site stop working. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non‑essential cookies. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser or by using the controls listed in section 3 above.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will always reflect the most recent change. Material changes will, where appropriate, be highlighted on the home page for a short period after they take effect.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, please reach us at souren.devapp@gmail.com.
