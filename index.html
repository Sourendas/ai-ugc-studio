HONEST ANSWERS

Questions brands ask before they book.

What exactly do I get when I order a Reel?

Every order includes a 15–30 second vertical Reel featuring your product and our consistent virtual creator, plus a written hook line, caption, and a set of relevant hashtags. The Test Drive and Monthly Presence packages are optimised for organic Reels; the Content Engine package can be unlocked for paid usage with a 60‑day licence.

Who owns the content once it’s delivered?

You do. After delivery and final payment, the Reel and its caption copy are yours to publish on your own channels. If you want to run the Reel as a paid ad on Meta, TikTok or YouTube, ask about our extended paid‑usage licence at checkout.

Will the virtual creator look the same across all my Reels?

Yes — that is the whole point of the studio. AYRA is a consistent on‑brand persona, so every Reel you receive in a single package, and every monthly package after that, features the same look, voice and styling. Your feed stops looking like a roulette of random AI faces.

What do you need from me to start?

Three to five clear product photos (front, back, label, in‑use if possible) and a one‑line description of what you want to sell. If you have an offer, a launch date, or a competitor whose Reels you admire, send that too. We will come back to you within one business day with a concept outline.

How fast is delivery?

The Test Drive package is delivered within five working days. Monthly Presence has a priority turnaround of roughly three to four days per Reel. The Content Engine plan runs on a shared monthly calendar that we agree together during onboarding.

Can I request a revision?

Every package includes at least one round of revisions per Reel. Most revisions are caption or hook copy tweaks; if you would like a full visual rework, we will quote it as an add‑on so you stay in control of the budget.

Do you write the captions in my brand voice?

We write the first draft in a neutral, conversion‑aware voice that fits Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Send us two or three captions you have already used and liked, and we will match that tone for everything we deliver.

Is this AI generated content, and is that safe for ads?

Yes, the Reels are produced with a combination of generative AI and human direction. We label the production process honestly in our terms, and we never pass AI work off as real customer footage. For Meta and TikTok paid ads we also offer a paid‑usage add‑on that covers the additional platform disclosure requirements.
